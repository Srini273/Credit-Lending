📊 CORE CALCULATIONS & FORMULAS

1. EMI Calculation (Equated Monthly Installment)
   Formula: EMI = [P × R × (1+R)^n] / [(1+R)^n - 1]
   Where:
   - P = Principal Loan Amount
   - R = Monthly Interest Rate (Annual Rate ÷ 12)
   - n = Number of Months
   
   Example: ₹10,00,000 @ 10% p.a. for 60 months = ₹21,247

2. FOIR - Fixed Obligation Income Ratio
   Formula: FOIR = (Total Monthly Obligations ÷ Monthly Income) × 100
   Safe Range: < 40%
   Acceptable: 40-55%
   Risk: 55-70%
   
   Example: EMI ₹21,247 + Existing ₹15,000 = ₹36,247 ÷ ₹75,000 = 48.3%

3. DSCR - Debt Service Coverage Ratio
   Formula: DSCR = (Monthly Income - Total Obligations) ÷ Proposed EMI
   Healthy: ≥ 1.25x
   Marginal: 1.0-1.25x
   Stressed: < 1.0x
   
   Example: (₹75,000 - ₹36,247) ÷ ₹21,247 = 1.82x (Healthy)

4. PTI - Payment-to-Income Ratio
   Formula: PTI = (Proposed EMI ÷ Monthly Income) × 100
   Safe: < 30%
   Moderate: 30-45%
   Risk: > 45%

5. LTI - Loan-to-Income Ratio
   Formula: LTI = Loan Amount ÷ Annual Income
   Conservative: < 2.0x
   Moderate: 2.0-3.5x
   Aggressive: > 3.5x

🎯 RISK SCORING ALGORITHM

Creditworthiness Score (0-100):
  
  Base: 50 points
  
  Add:
  + (CIBIL Score ÷ 900) × 20      [0-20 points]
  + FOIR Assessment              [5-20 points]  (Lower FOIR = More points)
  + DSCR Assessment              [0-15 points]  (Higher DSCR = More points)
  + Employment Stability          [3-10 points]  (Salaried = 10, Gig = 3)
  + Liquidity Buffer              [2-10 points]  (Strong AMB = 10)
  + Current Ratio                 [3-8 points]   (Higher CR = More points)
  + Bounce History                [0-7 points]   (Clean = 7, Frequent = 0)
  + Work Orders Pipeline          [0-5 points]   (Strong = 5)
  
  Final Score = Sum (capped at 100)

Risk Tiers:
  75-100 → Tier 1: APPROVE ✅ (Low Risk)
  55-74  → Tier 2: CONDITIONAL ⚠️ (Medium Risk)
  35-54  → Tier 3: MANUAL REVIEW 🔍 (High Risk)
  0-34   → Tier 4: DECLINE ❌ (Critical Risk)
